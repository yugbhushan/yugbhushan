## The engineering problem

Pressure drop constrains the operation of packed beds. Particle shape and wall effects can alter resistance and flow distribution, making them important considerations in bed design.

## Work completed

- Derived the Ergun equation from a shell momentum balance.
- Extended the treatment to non-spherical particles using sphericity.
- Built a Python pressure-drop model.
- Modelled wall channeling with the Benenati–Brosilow correlation.
- Proposed packed-bed design guidelines based on the analysis.

## Reported result

The CV reports approximately **190% higher pressure drop per unit length** for crushed catalyst with a sphericity of **0.65**, compared with spherical beads in the studied comparison. The result applies to that model comparison; it is not a universal percentage for all flow rates or beds.
